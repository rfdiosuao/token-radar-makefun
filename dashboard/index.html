How the implied value is worked out

implied = 10^(0.92 · log10 TVL) × growth × age × chains × sector × 0.35

Growth is the 7-day TVL change (unreported counts as flat). Age is days since first listing, capped at 4 years — a protocol that has run for years with no token is more likely to issue one. Chains adds 8% per extra chain, capped at 6. Sector is a per-category multiplier (Lending 1.18, DEX 1.15, down to Bridge 0.85). Nothing is hardcoded by name: the same formula scores every protocol. Snapshot date and floor are shown in the header.

Values are the radar's own claim from the sealed snapshot — not a prediction that a token will launch, or when. Farm only money you can afford to leave in place.
